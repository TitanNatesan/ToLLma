## 2. How to complain

Write to the Grievance Officer with your account email, the issue
category (privacy, data rights, content action appeal, billing), and
supporting references (request/report IDs). Complaints about AUP or
abuse actions should quote the report ID for the 7-state appeal track.

`retention-deletion-policy` (audit class). DSR-type grievances also enter
