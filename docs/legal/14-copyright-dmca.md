## 2. Takedown notice

Send: identification of the work, the infringing material (URLs/IDs),
your contact, good-faith statement, accuracy statement under penalty of
perjury, and signature. Deficient notices are returned with guidance
rather than acted on.

## 3. Counter-notice and restoration

Subjects may counter-notify with identification, consent to jurisdiction,
and acceptance of process; material is restored 10–14 business days after
a valid counter-notice unless the complainant files court action.

## 4. Repeat infringers

Accounts with repeated valid infringements are terminated. AI-generated
outputs that reproduce third-party works are handled under the same flow;
model-training disputes with upstream providers are directed to the
provider's own process (see `model-provider-disclosure`).
